§5.1 注记原文(v3,两栏排版已按阅读顺序拼回):

> GRB050803 — The host galaxy is marginally (3–5σ) detected in deep R-band imaging from the VLT, in i-band imaging from the GTC, and with the F160W (H-band) filter on HST, but no other wavelength (although a hint of a marginal detection is evident in the Spitzer imaging). The lack of g-band detection is indicative of a dropout at a redshift of z ∼ 4, although not an unambiguous one. Fitting the photometry using the EaZy photometric redshift software (Brammer et al. 2008) and disallowing passive low-redshift solutions indicates a redshift of z ∼ 4.3 (with ±0.4 redshift uncertainty at 1σ confidence, although 1.9 < z < 4.9 is permitted at 95% confidence.) This redshift is consistent with (but slightly higher than) the result of Schulze et al. 2015 using the same data (but different photo-z software).
